Quick answers

Before you book the audit.

Is this a full accounting package?

No. It is a lightweight invoicing, payment-request and chasing tool. It does not replace your bookkeeper, Sage or Xero. Many owners keep it for WhatsApp chasing and give the export to their bookkeeper.

Does it send WhatsApp messages for me?

No. It writes the message and opens a wa.me link with the amount, invoice number and PayShap or EFT details already filled in. You press send. That keeps you in control, and keeps the tool simple.

What about VAT?

If you are VAT-registered, switch on the 15% VAT toggle and the invoice shows VAT and a tax-invoice heading. If you are not registered, leave it off. This is a helper, not tax advice.

Where is my data stored?

On the device you use, in the browser. Export a JSON backup (full) or CSV (spreadsheet) regularly, especially before clearing browser data. You can import a backup to restore or move to another computer.
